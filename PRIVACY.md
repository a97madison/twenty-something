# Privacy Policy — 20·Something

_Last updated: June 2026._

The short version: we collect as little as possible and never sell it.

## What stays on your device
Your stats, streak, freezes, and rival records live only in the app's local
storage on your phone. You can erase all of it any time from **Settings → Delete
all my data**.

## What touches our server
When you finish a daily challenge, the app submits **only your star rating for
that date** so we can show your percentile against everyone who played. To do
that, the app signs in **anonymously** — there is no account, email, or password,
and the anonymous identifier is not tied to your real identity.

Friend challenges work entirely offline: the challenge code carries a random,
device-generated player id and the display name you optionally type — nothing
more.

## What we do NOT collect
No name (beyond an optional display name you choose), email, phone number,
contacts, or location. No advertising. No third-party analytics or trackers. We
do not sell or share your data with anyone.

## Children
The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone,
there is nothing that identifies a child.

## Your control
Delete all on-device data from Settings at any time. The anonymous server records
cannot be linked back to you.

## Contact
Questions? Reach out at the support address listed on the app's store page.
